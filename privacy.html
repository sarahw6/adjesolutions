Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information

Effective Date: January 1, 2025

Information We Collect

When you use our website, subscribe to our newsletter, or contact us through our forms, we may collect the following information:

  • Name: When you submit our contact form.
  • Email address: When you subscribe to our newsletter, download a free resource, or contact us.
  • Message content: When you submit our contact form.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and messages.
  • Send you our newsletter and free resources you have requested.
  • Improve our website and services.

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services such as email marketing platforms to deliver newsletters and resources. These services have their own privacy policies governing how they handle your data.

Cookies

Our website uses minimal cookies and local storage for functionality purposes, such as remembering whether you have dismissed a notification bar or already downloaded a resource. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

You may:

  • Unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time using the link in any email.
  • Request that we delete your personal information by emailing us.
  • Contact us with any questions about your data.

Changes to This Policy
